Hello! I'm **Armoghan-ul-Mohmin**, a **Digital Forensic and Cyber Security** graduate with hands-on experience in digital investigations, cybercrime analysis, incident response and security research through internship with [**Punjab Police**](https://www.punjabpolice.gov.pk/) and [**Digital Forensic Research and Service Center** (DFRSC)](https://pk.linkedin.com/company/dfrsc).

My interests span both [**Blue Team**](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_team_(computer_security)>) and [**Red Team**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_team#Cybersecurity) domains. I enjoy investigating security incidents, experimenting with detection engineering, learning defensive technologies such as SIEM and endpoint monitoring, and exploring offensive security techniques through ethical hacking labs and [**Capture The Flag (CTF)**](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_the_flag_(cybersecurity)>) competitions.

Outside of cybersecurity, I'm an [open-source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source) enthusiast who enjoys building projects, automating workflows, working in Linux environments, and continuously improving my technical skills. I believe the best way to learn is by building, breaking, analyzing, and documenting everything along the way.
